# Photo retouching notes

These are AI-retouched derivatives of actual café photographs, created with the built-in image generation tool. Source photographs are preserved in this repository. The Featured Dish still uses its original photograph.

## Shared prompt

Use case: lighting-weather and precise-object-edit. Edit the supplied real restaurant photograph for a Café Itadaki website food card. Make it appetizing with soft warm window light, correct neutral white balance, gentle open shadows, natural fresh texture, and a quiet warm light-oak tabletop background. Portrait 4:5 composition, dish fills about 75% of frame, enough margin to crop to a tall card. This is faithful photographic retouching: retain the EXACT actual food identity, ingredients, portions, number of pieces, rice/egg shape, and original white plate. Do not invent garnishes, toppings, sauces, fillings, steam, props or cutlery. No text, no logos, no watermark. Food must look real and modest, not exaggerated or glossy.
